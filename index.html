🏆 1st Place NASA NCAS Robotics Competition · 2026

GRIT — Mars Companion Rover

Perseverance can only sample the top 7 cm of Martian soil — too shallow for the purest science. Our answer: GRIT, a companion rover built to follow Perseverance and pull deeper, cleaner samples.

I contributed to the technical design of its hybrid rotary-percussive drill, reaching 200 cm — 28× deeper — at under 20% of the cost. With a 7-person team at Miami Dade College, we delivered a 21-section business proposal, built a working prototype, and presented to judges. We took 1st place.

  • Drill depth200 cm (vs. 7 cm)
  • Cost target< 20% of Perseverance's sampling system
  • DeliverablesWorking prototype + 21-section proposal
